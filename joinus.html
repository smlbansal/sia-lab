General Inquiries

In addition to the openings above, SIA Lab is always interested in connecting with researchers and students whose interests align with our work. Opportunities may depend on funding, timing, and mutual research fit.

Because of the high volume of inquiries, please use the guidance below before reaching out. A short, specific note is much easier to review than a long generic email.

Specific Research Questions

If you have technical questions about lab papers or projects, please feel free to email Somil with a subject line that includes research question. You may receive a faster response if you also email or CC the graduate student listed on the relevant paper.

Prospective PhD Students

Please apply to the Stanford AA PhD program and mention Somil Bansal under faculty of interest. Admissions are coordinated at the department level, so admission-specific questions and application-review requests should go to the department.

After submitting your application, you are welcome to send a concise email with your CV and 2-3 sentences about the research interests, projects, or papers that most excite you.

Prospective Postdoctoral Scholars

Excellent postdoctoral candidates are encouraged to contact Somil with:

  • academic CV and publication list
  • a short description of past and future research interests
  • names and contact information for two academic references

External fellowship applications are welcome, and the lab is happy to discuss potential fit and funding paths.

Current UG/MS Students, Visiting Students, and Interns

If you are interested in joining or visiting the lab, please send a concise email with:

  • CV and transcripts
  • a brief note on why you are interested in SIA Lab and which projects interest you
  • relevant background in ML, control, robotics, Python, deep learning, ROS, or hardware
  • desired duration and whether you need funding or have external funding
